Privacy Policy

Upload Scheduler · Last updated 15 November 2026

Upload Scheduler is a private tool used only by its owner to upload, schedule and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel, currently Survival Extremes (formerly Wild Biology), and to read that channel's own statistics. This policy explains what data it accesses and what it does with it.

Data the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google sign-in, the tool uses four permissions. Each is used only on the owner's own channel:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload the channel owner's approved videos with their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's name and ID and the status of its own videos.
  • See, edit and permanently delete your YouTube videos, ratings, comments and captions (youtube.force-ssl): to change the publish time, title, description, playlists and subtitle tracks of the owner's own videos, and to read comments on those videos and post replies the owner has written or approved. Although Google's permission allows deleting, the tool never deletes videos, comments or captions, never rates videos, and never posts anything the owner has not approved.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports for your YouTube content (yt-analytics.readonly): to read the channel's own aggregated statistics, such as views, average percentage viewed, audience retention, subscribers gained and traffic sources.

It does not access other channels, any viewer's private information or any other Google service.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload, schedule and manage the channel owner's own videos, to reply to comments on them with replies the owner approves, and to help the owner plan future videos from the channel's own statistics.

Comments are read only on the owner's own videos and only to draft replies; the commenters' names and words are not used for anything else. Analytics are aggregated channel statistics and contain no information about individual viewers. The owner may use an AI assistant to draft replies and summarise statistics, acting only on the owner's behalf; this tool does not use the data to train AI models. Nothing is used for advertising or for profiling individual people, and nothing is ever sold or shared with anyone.

Storage and security

The only stored credential is a Google authorization token, kept as a private secret in the channel owner's own cloud environment and used only by this tool. Video files and their text are kept in the channel owner's private project files. Upload results (video ID, schedule) are stored next to each video. Comment text and analytics figures fetched from YouTube are kept in those private files only while they are needed, and are refreshed or deleted within 30 days.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page. Once revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the cloud environment. Videos already uploaded stay on the YouTube channel, where they can be managed or deleted in YouTube Studio.
