Upload Scheduler

A private tool that schedules Shorts on its owner's own YouTube channel, currently Wild Biology, after the owner has reviewed and approved each one.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video to the Wild Biology channel as a private video with a scheduled publish time, together with its title, description, tags and AI-content disclosure.
  • Reads the channel's name and the upload status of its own videos, to confirm that an upload worked.

Every video is written, checked and approved by the channel owner before it is uploaded. The tool never uploads anything that has not been approved, and it does not comment, like, subscribe, delete videos or access any other channel.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Wild Biology channel. It is not offered to the public and nobody else can sign in to it.
