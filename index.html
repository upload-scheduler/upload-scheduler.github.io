Upload Scheduler

A private tool that schedules and manages Shorts on its owner's own YouTube channel, currently Survival Extremes (formerly Wild Biology), after the owner has reviewed and approved each one.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video to the Survival Extremes channel as a private video with a scheduled publish time, together with its title, description, tags and AI-content disclosure.
  • Manages the channel's own videos when the owner asks: changes their publish time, title, description, playlists and subtitles.
  • Reads comments on the channel's own videos and posts a reply only after the owner has written or approved it.
  • Reads the channel's own analytics, such as views, retention, subscribers gained and traffic sources, to help the owner plan new videos.
  • Reads the channel's name and the status of its own videos, to confirm that an upload worked.

Every video is written, checked and approved by the channel owner before it is uploaded. The tool never uploads or posts anything the owner has not approved, never deletes videos or comments, does not like or subscribe, and does not access any other channel.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Survival Extremes channel. It is not offered to the public and nobody else can sign in to it.
